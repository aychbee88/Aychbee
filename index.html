The ritual

Your moment of indulgence

  1. Prepare

    Begin with freshly cleansed, dry skin around the eyes.

  2. Lift

    With tweezers or clean fingertips, gently lift one patch at a time.

  3. Unwind

    Smooth each patch beneath the eye, then sit back and savour 10–15 minutes of calm.

  4. Reveal

    Peel away and press the remaining serum into the skin to reveal a refreshed, radiant gaze.
